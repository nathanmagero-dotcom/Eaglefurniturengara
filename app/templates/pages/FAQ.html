Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our furniture, custom orders, delivery, payments, warranties and services.

General Questions

Eagle Furniture Ngara offers a wide range of furniture including sofas, L-shaped sofas, beds, wardrobes, dining sets, TV stands, wall units, coffee tables, office furniture and other home furnishings.

We also offer custom-made furniture according to your preferred design, size, colour and finishing.

We are located on Murang'a Road in Ngara, next to Kahama Hotel, Nairobi, Kenya. Customers can visit our workshop to discuss furniture requirements, review available materials and discuss custom-made furniture with our team.

Yes. You can browse our furniture collection online and place an order or request a quotation. For made-to-order furniture, our team confirms the design, colour, materials, specifications, price and delivery arrangements with you before production begins.

Custom Furniture

Yes. We specialize in bespoke furniture. You can provide your preferred design, measurements, colour, fabric or reference image and our team can help create furniture suited to your space and style.

Production time depends on the type, quantity and complexity of the furniture. After the design, colour, materials, specifications and price are agreed, our team confirms the expected production timeline and delivery arrangements before production begins.

Yes. Depending on the furniture design, customers can select preferred fabrics, colours, finishes and other customization options. Our team can guide you in selecting combinations that work well with your space.

Pricing & Payment

Prices vary depending on the furniture type, size, materials, design and customization. You can browse our products for current prices or contact us for a quotation.

For made-to-order and customized furniture, a 60% deposit is required after the design, colour, materials, specifications and price have been agreed.

The remaining balance is payable on delivery. The deposit confirms the order and secures the agreed production and delivery arrangements.

Yes. We offer complete furniture packages designed to help customers furnish rooms, apartments, Airbnb properties, offices and entire homes.

From time to time, we run special offers, seasonal promotions and furniture package discounts. Visit our Offers page to see current promotions.

Delivery & Installation

Yes. We can arrange delivery to customers outside Nairobi. Delivery is free within Nairobi. For deliveries outside Nairobi, we can arrange delivery and the customer assists with the applicable transport cost depending on the destination and furniture requirements.

Installation support may be available depending on the type of furniture and delivery arrangement. Please confirm installation requirements when placing your order.

Delivery is free within Nairobi. For deliveries outside Nairobi, the customer assists with the applicable transport cost. The final transport arrangement depends on the destination and furniture requirements and is confirmed before delivery.

Warranty & Quality

Warranty coverage depends on the product and order. Please confirm the applicable warranty terms with our team before completing your purchase.

Contact our team as soon as possible after delivery and provide details and photographs of the issue. We will assess the situation and advise you on the appropriate solution.
