WHO WE ARE

More Than Furniture. We Create Spaces.

Eagle Furniture Ngara is a furniture and interior design company based in Nairobi, Kenya.

We believe that furniture should do more than fill an empty room. It should reflect your personality, complement your lifestyle and make your space feel truly yours.

From a statement sofa for your living room to a complete interior transformation, we work with our customers to create spaces that are beautiful, practical and built to last.

Whether you are furnishing your home, office, Airbnb, apartment, restaurant or commercial space, our team can help you from concept to completion.

OUR STORY

Built Around Quality, Creativity and Trust.

Eagle Furniture Ngara was built with a simple vision: to make quality furniture and beautiful interior spaces more accessible to customers in Kenya.

We understand that furnishing a home or business is a major investment. That is why we focus on combining quality workmanship, thoughtful design and customer-focused service.

Our goal is not simply to sell you a product. We want to understand what you need, recommend the right solution and help you create a space you will be proud of.
